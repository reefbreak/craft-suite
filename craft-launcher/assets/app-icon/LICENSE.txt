The Gesso name and mark identify Gesso and Gesso Launcher. A fork that is not Gesso should use its
own name and icon. The upstream ArtCraft Launcher's icon (the ArtCraft "A" mark) is an ArtCraft
trademark and is not included in this build: docs/brand/README.md.
